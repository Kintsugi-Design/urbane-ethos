Sharing time!

Some parents might be worried about the future of their child with developmental delays.

Here is a truly living example of a one courageous past client and his family.

With the permission of their parents, we just want to share the achievement of the kiddos that we had worked with in the past. Kudos to the parents who are heros and practice proactive parenting. They were willing to be open and patiently work with us and tried their best without any excuses of being busy despite having a grueling and demanding professional career.

AR was diagnosed with apraxia of speech or better known as speech delay. We still remember how difficult it was for AR to catch and throw a ball or to say his first words and to speak and to learn. We are humbled by his vast progress year after year and now he has achieved so much. Now he can handle himself in a boarding school and exploring various interests in sports and music.

We are so proud of you, AR! We salute your parents and your whole family especially your grandma who tirelessly support you.

Thank you for always keeping in touch by sending us updates of AR's achievements and making us therapists' feel all that hardwork is truly worth it. We are proud to see how far AR has come and how he has bloomed to be a handsome and capable teenager.

Love you AR and our prayers and best wishes are always with you!
