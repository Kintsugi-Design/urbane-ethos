Bravo parents for attending and participating in EIP parenting class to learn more about sensory integration processing and fine motor skills. We are proud to see both parents are joining forces and coming up with activities to be done at home with their child. Our parents are our true heros and advocators. Thank you for entrusting us to assist you. See you again for progress report and individual consultation with our therapists and teacher soon.
