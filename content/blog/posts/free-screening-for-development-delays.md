Every month, the MBI State Selangor and Persatuan Taska Negeri Selangor through collaboration with Urbane Ethos Early Intervention Center open their doors to kids aged 3 years and below who are suspected of having developmental delays but have not yet consulted any specialists due to reasons such as those related to financials. We invite families with combined household income not exceeding RM5,000 per month (if exceeds it depends on the number of dependants).

### Ujian Saringan Percuma Bagi Kelewatan Perkembangan

Setiap bulan, MBI Selangor serta Persatuan Taska Selangor dengan kerjasama pihak Urbane Ethos Early Intervention Center membuka pintu kepada kanak-kanak yang berumur 3 tahun ke bawah, yang disyaki mempunyai kelewatan perkembangan tetapi belum pertemu apa-apa pakar dan pendapatan seisi rumah tidak melebihi RM5,000 sebulan (Jika melebihi, bergantung kepada jumlah tangunggan).
