Dear parents,

No one would predict this predicament as everyone is affected by Covid-19 pandemic. As those who work closely with you, we do understand the challenges you might be facing while staying home while multi-tasking with work and having to care for everyone of your family.

On top of it all, you might have these worries:

Your child might be regressing while you are now worried about what to do and how to help your child who is either undergoing treatment.

Perhaps you are concerned that your child is still unable to read and understand. He has yet to learn about concepts and is way behind others in his kindergarten class.

Or that he is playing all alone and not wanting to be engaged by you or others. The uncertainty of what would be happening in the near future also looms ahead.

Or that he is manifesting more tantrums and behaviors now more than ever as everyone has been cooped up for so long.
