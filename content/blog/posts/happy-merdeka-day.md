Updated: Sep 27, 2019

Happy Merdeka Day to all Malaysians!

The different conditions, diseases we face in our lifetime and the difficulty to overcome them knows no race. So why should we let the colour of our skin define who we are and who we choose to help?

Love and empathy for all creations is important. Extend a helping hand to those around you despite of their background because we are all more similar that we think.

Love each other. Love all Malaysians because this is our Home. Home is where you can go the local store or eatery and call each other 'aunty/mak cik/abang/uncle/kakak' like family.

Happy holidays and we wish everyone a safe trip back home this weekend!

Note: UE Center will be closed Today and on Monday, 2/9/2019 for celebrations
