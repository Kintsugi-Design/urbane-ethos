Today was jammed packed!

In the morning we opened our door to Speech Language Pathologist, Lauren Reyes-Yu, M.A. CCC from Ohio State University to give us some input on Alternative Augmented Communications such as sign language and PECs that can be used to communicate with children who have communication disorders!

![Speech-language pathologist leading an AAC input session at Urbane Ethos](./assets/img/blog/saturdays-at-ue/02.jpg)

In the evening, Urbane Ethos Early Intervention Center collaborated with Qaisworks Production as one of the shooting location for an up and coming TV3 drama series "Wasiat Dari Jordon" featuring a pair of lovers and an autistic child.

![Behind the scenes of the TV3 drama shoot at Urbane Ethos](./assets/img/blog/saturdays-at-ue/03.jpg)

We look forward to more collaborations in the future!

![Cast and crew during the drama series collaboration](./assets/img/blog/saturdays-at-ue/04.jpg)

Note: Tomorrow (20th October) and next month in November are our final two sessions for 2019 for the Free EIP Screening program and is still open for registrations. Do register first as any slots are booked via appointments only!
