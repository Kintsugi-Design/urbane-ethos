Sunday 17/11/2019: Today Urbane Ethos concluded the final 8th free screening exercise for state of Selangor with collaboration with #MBI and #PersatuanTaskaSelangor . A multidisciplinary team of experienced and capable specialists and allied health professionals teamed up to serve the families within Selangor who had signed up and qualified for the free screening. Kudos to the cheerful and energetic colleagues of pediatricians, FMS, audiologists, physiotherapists, occupational therapists, clinical linguist, speech therapists and parenting counselor who had gathered together once a month and sacrified their Sunday to make this happen. This is #UrbaneEthos 's 4th year, and for some 2nd and 3rd year involving ourselves in this endeavour. It has been quite a journey with over 500 kids screened and chanelled for diagnosis and intervention.

We all feel very happy and blessed to be able to help.

Thank you for this opportunity to make a difference in the lives of future generation and future leaders. 💖💖💖
