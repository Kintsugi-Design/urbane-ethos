The Early Intervention Program or EIP is a therapy-based group program that is highly individualized to each child and focuses on a client-centered approach while also facilitating social interaction and communication with their peers!

Lead by our trained therapists, EIP group also includes:

- Occupational therapy such as sensory play, cognitive and motor development, activities of daily living (ADL)
- Speech Therapy
- Physical and outdoor activities
- Arts and Crafts!
