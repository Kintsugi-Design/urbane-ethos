Empower Young Minds: Join Our Compassionate Team and together let us Make a Difference!

2023-10-19 · 2 min read

Embark on a Rewarding Journey at Urbane Ethos Early Intervention Center!

Are you a dedicated professional seeking a meaningful career in making a positive impact on the lives of children and teenagers with special needs? Join our dynamic team at Urbane Ethos Early Intervention Center, where part-time and full-time opportunities await in the following roles:

  • Occupational Therapist
  • Physiotherapist
  • Speech-Language Pathologist
  • Special Education Teacher
  • Teachers aid or Therapist assistant
  • Administrator

About Urbane Ethos Early Intervention Center: At Urbane Ethos, we’re not just a center; we’re a community committed to providing comprehensive support for children and teenagers aged 18 years and below. Our multidisciplinary approach ensures that each child receives personalized care tailored to their unique needs.

Why Choose Urbane Ethos

  • Collaborative Environment: Join a team that values collaboration and encourages the exchange of innovative ideas. We believe in working together to create the best outcomes for our young learners.
  • Holistic Approach: Be part of an organization that focuses on holistic intervention. Our team is dedicated to nurturing the physical, emotional, and educational development of every child.
  • Professional Growth: Urbane Ethos is dedicated to the professional growth of its team members. We provide opportunities for continuous learning, training, and skill development.

Your Role at Urbane Ethos: As a member of our team, you will play a crucial role in planning and providing interventions in both group and individual settings. We are looking for individuals who not only have a passion for working with children but also bring fresh perspectives and are enthusiastic about collaborative efforts within the team.

Qualifications

  • Relevant degree and certifications in your respective field.
  • Previous experience working with children or teenagers with special needs is preferred.
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills.

How to Apply: If you are ready to make a difference in the lives of children and contribute to a supportive and collaborative environment, we invite you to apply. Send your resume, cover letter, and relevant certifications to urbaneethos@yahoo.com.

Join Urbane Ethos Early Intervention Center and be part of a team that is dedicated to empowering the potential of every child we serve.
