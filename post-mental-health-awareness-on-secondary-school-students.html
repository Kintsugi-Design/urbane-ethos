Mental Health Awareness on Secondary School Students

2024-06-07 · 4 min read

This comprehensive study highlights several key findings regarding the influence of familiarity, media exposure, knowledge, and attitude on mental health awareness among secondary school students. Familiarity with mental health issues and professional help positively influences knowledge and attitudes toward mental health, which, in turn, enhances mental health awareness. Media exposure also plays a significant role in improving knowledge and attitudes, thereby positively impacting mental health awareness. However, the study suggests that knowledge on professional help did not mediate the relationship between familiarity or media exposure and mental health awareness, indicating a need for more targeted education and awareness efforts in this area.

Overall, fostering positive attitudes and increasing knowledge through various channels can contribute to greater mental health awareness among youth. In the article, it states that Schools also play a crucial role in promoting mental health awareness among students.

Here’s how:

Education and Curriculum Integration: Schools can integrate mental health education into their curriculum, teaching students about the importance of mental health, common mental health disorders, coping mechanisms, and how to seek help. By incorporating mental health topics into subjects like health education or psychology, students gain essential knowledge and skills to manage their mental well-being.

Awareness Campaigns and Events: Schools can organize awareness campaigns, events, and workshops focused on mental health. These events provide platforms for discussions, raise awareness about mental health issues, reduce stigma, and promote help-seeking behaviors. Activities like mental health assemblies, guest speaker sessions, and peer support groups foster an open dialogue around mental health.

Access to Resources: Schools should provide access to mental health resources and support services. This includes having trained counselors or mental health professionals available for students to talk to, establishing mental health hotlines or peer support programs, and offering referrals to community mental health services when needed. Accessible resources ensure that students have the support they need when facing mental health challenges.

Promotion of Positive School Environment: Schools can create a supportive and inclusive environment that prioritizes mental well-being. This involves promoting positive relationships among students and staff, implementing anti-bullying policies, fostering a sense of belonging and acceptance, and providing opportunities for physical activity and relaxation. A positive school environment contributes to students’ overall mental health and reduces stress.

Teacher Training and Support: Schools should provide training and support for teachers and staff to recognize signs of mental distress in students and respond effectively. Training programs can educate staff about mental health issues, teach strategies for supporting students’ emotional well-being, and provide guidance on referring students to appropriate services. Empowered teachers play a vital role in early intervention and support for students’ mental health needs.

Peer Support and Mentoring Programs: Schools can implement peer support and mentoring programs where older students or trained peers support their classmates’ mental health. Peer support initiatives create a sense of community, reduce feelings of isolation, and encourage students to seek help from their peers, fostering a culture of mutual support and understanding.

Parent and Caregiver Involvement: Schools should involve parents and caregivers in mental health education initiatives. Parent workshops, information sessions, and newsletters can provide resources and strategies for supporting children’s mental well-being at home.

Collaborating with families ensures a holistic approach to promoting mental health awareness and support for students. By actively engaging with mental health education, creating supportive environments, and providing access to resources and support services, schools can play a pivotal role in promoting mental health awareness and fostering positive mental well-being among students.

Parents, school teachers and counsellors, as well as allied health professionals must work together to help the children of today to enable them to be independent, kind and strong in the future.
